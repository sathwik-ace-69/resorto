Best Resorts For Holidays in Goa

Taj Cidade de Goa Horizon

Situated in Panaji, right on the tranquil Vanguinim Beach Dona Paula in North Goa, Taj Resort & Convention Centre Goa features luxurious rooms offering suitable choices to guests. With only 28 km and 45 mins drive from the Dabolim airport the hotel is located in the heart of Goa.

From ₹10000 per night

Palma Beach Resort

Situated in Mandrem, a few steps from Ashwem Beach, Palma Beach Resort features accommodation with a garden, free private parking, a private beach area and a terrace. Featuring a bar, the property is located within 16 km of Chapora Fort.

From ₹14000 per night

The Coast Beach Resort

Located in Agonda, 300 metres from Agonda Beach, The Coast Beach Resort provides accommodation with a garden, free private parking, a private beach area and a restaurant. The property is set 41 km from Netravali Wildlife Sanctuary and 46 km from Mother of God Church.

From ₹17000 per night

The Postcard Hideaway, Netravali Wildlife Sanctuary, Goa

Set in Vichondrem, 43 km from Margao Railway Station.This property offers accommodation with a garden, free private parking, a shared lounge and a restaurant. 1.2 km from Netravali Wildlife Sanctuary.

From ₹30000 per night

Storii By ITC Hotels, Shanti Morada Goa

Situated in Calangute, 11 km from Chapora Fort, Storii By ITC Hotels Shanti Morada Saligao features accommodation with an outdoor swimming pool, free private parking, a garden, and shared lounges.

From ₹13500 per night

Ronil Goa, part of Hyatt

Located on the iconic Calangute-Baga Road in North Goa, Ronil Goa is a boutique resort on this bustling stretch, well connected to key tourist attractions, upscale shopping, night-clubs and entertainment. Welcome to Ronil Goa, a place that is like your weekend mood.

From ₹21000 per night

Le Meridien Goa, Calangute

Offering an outdoor pool and fitness centre, Le Méridien Goa, Calangute is located in Calangute, 800 metres from Calangute beach. The hotel has a terrace and views of the city.Latest Recipe our all day dining restaurant showcasing an irresistible visual and culinary spread.

From ₹11000 per night

Meritas Auberge Calangute Goa

Meritas Auberge Calangute Goa in Calangute offers family rooms with air-conditioning, private bathrooms, and balconies. Each room includes a tea and coffee maker, minibar, and free WiFi. Guests enjoy a rooftop swimming pool, sun terrace, and lush garden.

From ₹15500 per night
